
# Customer Analytics Domain

## Overview
This folder will house projects focused on understanding customer behaviour, predicting churn, and maximising customer lifetime value. The goal is to move beyond basic reporting and build predictive models that help businesses retain their most valuable segments.

## Planned Projects
- **Churn Prediction**: Binary classification models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost) to identify at-risk customers 30–60 days in advance.
- **Customer Segmentation**: RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis combined with K-Means clustering for targeted marketing campaigns.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Probabilistic models (BG/NBD, Gamma-Gamma) to estimate future revenue per customer.

## Tools & Techniques
- **Python**: pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels, lifetimes library.
- **Visualisation**: Seaborn, Plotly for cohort retention heatmaps.
- **Evaluation**: ROC-AUC, Precision-Recall, Lift curves.
